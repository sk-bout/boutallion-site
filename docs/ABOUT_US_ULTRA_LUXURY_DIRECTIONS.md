# About Us Page – Ultra-High-Net-Worth Brand Directions

Alternative directions to make the About Us page feel like an ultra-high-net-worth brand, beyond the current layout.

---

## 1. **Editorial / Magazine Layout**

- Large serif headlines, editorial grid, narrow columns.
- Mix of full-bleed images and asymmetric grids.
- Long-form, magazine-style text with drop caps and subtle typographic hierarchy.
- Scroll-driven editorial pacing.

---

## 2. **Immersive Full-Screen Sections**

- Full-viewport sections that snap or scroll with smooth transitions.
- One core idea per section.
- Strong use of imagery and minimal copy.
- Parallax or layered motion for depth.
- Clean, cinematic pacing.

---

## 3. **WebGL / Three.js Experience**

- 3D materials, soft lighting, reflections.
- Slow camera moves or subtle orbiting.
- Typography integrated into 3D space.
- "Jewellery-like" feel for key visuals.
- Luxury brand look and feel without feeling gamified.

---

## 4. **Horizontal Scroll Gallery**

- Horizontal scroll instead of vertical.
- Cards or panels slide in and out.
- Generous whitespace and focused content.
- Similar to high-end lookbooks or flagship sites.

---

## 5. **Micro-Interactions & Cursor**

- Custom cursor that responds to elements and hover.
- Hover states on images (tilt, slight scale, overlay).
- Smooth text reveals and line-by-line animations.
- Gold accents or light effects on hover.
- Polished, high-end feel through small details.

---

## 6. **Kinetic Typography**

- Headlines and quotes animate as they enter view.
- Split-text or letter-by-letter reveals.
- Sophisticated easing and subtle motion.
- Strong storytelling impact for key messages.

---

## 7. **Split-Screen / Dual-View**

- Content on one side, image or video on the other.
- Interaction between sides (hover, scroll).
- Clear hierarchy, uncluttered layout.
- Feels like a private viewing or consultation.

---

## 8. **Dark Mode Luxury**

- Very dark base (e.g. #0a0a0a) with gold or warm accents.
- Soft gradients and light glows.
- Frosted glass or glassmorphism panels.
- High contrast, minimal, premium feel.

---

## 9. **Scroll-Triggered Video**

- Background or hero video that plays on scroll.
- Subtle motion graphics for key messages.
- Video treated like editorial imagery, not autoplay noise.
- Strong narrative and emotional impact.

---

## 10. **"Private Salon" Vibe**

- Subtle entrance animation (curtain / fade in).
- Discrete, minimal UI; navigation hidden until needed.
- Slow, controlled scroll.
- Optional: blur / overlay on first visit that clears on interaction.
- Feels exclusive and invitation-only.

---

## Technologies to Support This

- **Framer Motion** – scroll-linked and layout animations.
- **GSAP + ScrollTrigger** – complex scroll and timeline animations.
- **Three.js / React Three Fiber** – 3D and WebGL.
- **Lenis** – smooth scrolling (already in use).
- **Locomotive Scroll** – smooth scroll and section effects.
- **Framer** – rich page transitions and interactions.
- **CSS Scroll-Driven Animations** – native, no-JS scroll effects (new spec).
- **View Transitions API** – native page and section transitions.

---

## Suggested Direction for Boutallion

A combination that fits an ultra-high-net-worth brand:

1. **Editorial layout** – strong typography, clear hierarchy.
2. **Micro-interactions and custom cursor** – subtle, premium feel.
3. **Kinetic typography** – key lines animate on scroll.
4. **Dark luxury palette** – dark base, gold accents, soft gradients.
5. **GSAP ScrollTrigger** – precise, scroll-linked motion.
6. **Lenis** – smooth, controlled scrolling.
